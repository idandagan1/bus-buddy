Common questions

The alarm didn't ring. Make sure location access is set to "Always" and notifications are allowed for Bus Buddy in Settings.

Times look wrong. Pull down to refresh. Live times come from Coach USA and NJ Transit and can lag behind the bus by a minute or two.

I don't see my stop. Search by street or town name, or by the stop number on the sign at an NJ Transit stop.
