Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Bus Buddy has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. The website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you. This page explains what the app uses and why.

Location

With your permission, Bus Buddy uses your location to show how far you are from your stops and to find places you search for. If you set a wake-up alarm, it also uses your location in the background, only while that alarm is set, to wake you before your stop. Background use stops when the alarm rings, when you cancel it, or a few hours after it was set.

Your location stays on your phone. It is not sent to us or stored on any server. In the app, place searches use your phone's built-in geocoder; on the website, the text you search for is sent through our server to OpenStreetMap's search service.

Your routes and settings

Saved routes, alarms, and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Schedule and live data

To show times, the app asks our server for the routes and stops you choose. The server fetches schedules and live status from Coach USA and NJ Transit and returns them. These requests contain stop and trip identifiers, not your identity or location. Like any web server, our hosting provider (Expo) may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time for security and operations.

Website visit counts

getbusbuddy.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see roughly where visitors come from (country, device type, the site that linked here). It sets no cookies, doesn't fingerprint your device, and doesn't track you across sites. The iPhone app has no analytics.

Notifications and alarms

Notifications and alarms are scheduled on your device. Bus Buddy does not use push notifications from a server.

Children

Bus Buddy is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions? See Support.
